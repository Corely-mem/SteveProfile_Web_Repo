WHO IS

STEVE?

Steve is the default male player character (avatar)
and original protagonist of the sandbox game Minecraft,
created by Markus "Notch" Persson and first appearing
in 2009. He is designed as a blank slate with no official
backstory, allowing players to project their own identity
onto him, though he is visually characterized by his cyan
shirt, purple pants, brown hair, and blocky voxel aesthetic.
